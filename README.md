# 🌾 AgriShield – Smart Agriculture Marketplace

**An AI + Blockchain-powered platform for a transparent, efficient, and farmer-centric agricultural supply chain.**


---

## 🚀 Overview

AgriShield is a technology-driven agricultural marketplace designed to reduce the dependency on multiple intermediaries between farmers and consumers.

The platform aims to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers through a unified digital ecosystem.

By combining Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, and smart logistics, AgriShield aims to improve farmers' market access, provide transparent transactions, and make agricultural supply chains more efficient.

## ❗ Problem Statement

Multiple intermediaries in agricultural supply chains reduce farmers' earnings while increasing prices for consumers.

Key challenges:
- Limited direct market access for farmers and FPOs.
- Demand uncertainty leading to wastage and price fluctuations.
- Fragmented orders and inefficient transportation.
- Lack of transparent product traceability.
- Limited visibility across the agricultural supply chain.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

AgriShield addresses these challenges through five major components:

### 1. Direct Agricultural Marketplace
- Enables farmers and FPOs to list their products.
- Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Promotes transparent pricing.

### 2. AI-Based Demand Prediction
- Forecasts agricultural product demand.
- Helps match buyers with suppliers.
- Supports better supply planning.

### 3. Smart Logistics
- Consolidates orders from nearby farmers.
- Optimizes delivery routes.
- Improves transportation efficiency.

### 4. Blockchain + QR Traceability
- Maintains transparent transaction records.
- Provides QR-based product batch traceability.
- Improves trust and product verification.

### 5. Unified Digital Platform
- Connects farmers, buyers, and logistics providers.
- Provides a centralized marketplace experience.

## 🛠️ Proposed Technology Stack

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| React.js | Frontend marketplace |
| FastAPI (Python) | Backend APIs |
| Scikit-learn / PyTorch | AI and demand forecasting |

| PostgreSQL | Database |

*Note: This is the proposed technology stack. Actual implementation may vary depending on the prototype.*

## 🔄 System Workflow

1. Farmer registration and product listing.
2. Products become visible on the marketplace.
3. Buyers browse products and place orders.
4. AI forecasts demand and matches buyers with suppliers.
5. Orders from nearby farmers are consolidated.
6. Logistics planning and route optimization are performed.
7. Blockchain records important transaction events.
8. QR codes enable product batch verification.
